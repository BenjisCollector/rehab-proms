# Clinical safety hazard log

Written in the style of a DCB0129 hazard log to show how clinical risk is tied to design and tests. **This is not a DCB0129 safety case.** No Clinical Safety Officer has reviewed it and the product is not deployed.

Risk = severity x likelihood, using the DCB0129 matrix (severity: Minor, Significant, Considerable, Major, Catastrophic; likelihood: Very low, Low, Medium, High, Very high; risk rating 1 to 5). Ratings below are the author's illustrative estimates.

| ID | Hazard | Cause | Effect and possible harm | Initial risk | Controls in this codebase | Residual risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-01 | Patient discloses thoughts of self-harm and nobody acts in time | PHQ-9 item 9 answered above zero but total score is low, so a total-only rule would miss it; dashboard not watched in real time | Delayed response to suicide risk. Catastrophic | Catastrophic x Medium = 4 | Item 9 rule independent of total (`src/lib/fhir/scoring.ts`, `PHQ9_ITEM9`); urgent alerts sort first (`caseload()` in `src/lib/repo.ts`); patient sees 999, NHS 111 and Samaritans signposting immediately, driven by stored data not the URL (`src/app/patient/page.tsx`). Tests: `tests/scoring.test.ts` "raises an urgent alert whenever PHQ-9 item 9...", `e2e/happy-path.spec.ts`, `e2e/access-control.spec.ts` "crisis signposting is driven by stored answers" | Catastrophic x Low = 3. Software cannot guarantee response time: the service must define who checks alerts and how often | Open: needs a service-level operating procedure |
| H-02 | Deterioration to the severe range is not flagged | Wrong threshold, off-by-one at band boundary | Missed escalation. Major | Major x Medium = 3 | `SEVERE_THRESHOLD` in one place; boundary tests at 14/15 and 19/20 | Major x Very low = 2 | Thresholds must be agreed by clinicians |
| H-03 | Wrong total score displayed | Hand-copied scoring weights, partial answers scored as if complete, client-supplied total trusted | Wrong severity band drives wrong decision. Major | Major x Medium = 3 | Weights read from the official Questionnaire `ordinalValue` extensions; `score()` throws on any missing or unknown answer; server recomputes total and validation rejects a mismatched total. Tests: "refuses to score an incomplete set", "rejects a tampered total" | Major x Very low = 2 | Closed for this scope |
| H-04 | Alert closed against the wrong patient, or closed twice | Form tampering, double submit, stale page | Real alert silently disappears. Major | Major x Low = 3 | `markAlertReviewed` requires the alert to belong to the patient in the URL and to be unreviewed; every review audited with outcome. Test: "only reviews an alert once, and only for the right patient" | Major x Very low = 2 | Closed for this scope |
| H-05 | Clinician acts on the wrong patient's record | Similar names, no strong identifier on screen | Treatment decision based on another person's data. Major | Major x Low = 3 | Name and identifier shown in the heading of every patient page; FHIR export carries identifiers; demo IDs instead of free-text matching | Major x Low = 3 | Open: real deployment must show NHS number and date of birth verified via PDS (see proms-fhir-bridge) |
| H-06 | Patient data disclosed to the wrong person | Missing authorisation check on one route, guessable IDs, shared device left signed in | Breach of confidentiality, loss of trust. Considerable | Considerable x Medium = 3 | Role check in every page, Server Action and route; object-level rule `canAccessPatient`; 404 for other patients' records; 8 hour session expiry; lockout after 5 failures; every access audited. Tests: `e2e/access-control.spec.ts`, `tests/repo-auth.test.ts` | Considerable x Low = 2 | Open: no MFA or idle timeout yet |
| H-07 | Patient is falsely reassured or alarmed by their own score | Scores shown without clinical context | Patient delays seeking help, or distress. Significant | Significant x Medium = 2 | Patients are not shown totals or bands; confirmation says answers are not checked straight away | Significant x Low = 2 | Decision to be revisited with clinicians and patient panel |
| H-08 | Downstream system receives malformed or misleading FHIR data | Resource built incorrectly, synthetic data mistaken for real | Wrong data in another clinical record. Major | Major x Low = 3 | Every `QuestionnaireResponse` validated before it is stored; Observations LOINC-coded with `derivedFrom`; `HTEST` tag on exported Patients; contract tests in proms-fhir-bridge | Major x Very low = 2 | Closed for this scope |

## Change control

Any pull request that touches `src/lib/fhir/`, `src/questionnaires/` or alert logic must reference the hazard IDs it affects and update this log (see the PR template).
