# 4. Patients do not see their own scores

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: Proposed default, needs clinical review.

## Context
Showing a PHQ-9 total and a label such as "Severe" to a patient with no clinician present can cause distress or false reassurance (hazard H-07).

## Decision
Patients see what they submitted and when, not totals or severity bands. If item 9 is flagged they see crisis signposting immediately.

## Consequences
- Lower risk of harm from unsupported interpretation.
- Some patients value seeing progress. This should be decided with clinicians and a patient panel, which is why the status is "proposed".
